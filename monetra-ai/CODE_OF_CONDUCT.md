# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim for a welcoming, respectful project where participants can contribute regardless of experience or background.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback and credit collaborators.
- Respect privacy and keep synthetic demo data separate from personal information.
- Focus disagreement on technical ideas, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory comments, intimidation, deliberate disruption, or publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer via GitHub's contact options. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers will review reports fairly and take proportionate action, including removing comments or limiting participation when necessary.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, discussions, reviews, and project-organized communication.
